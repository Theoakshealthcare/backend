## What shipped

Every cancelled paid booking is now either served or **refunded by a person's decision**. All refunds are rows in one queue (`refunds`), a human approves every one, it pays out exactly once through exactly one destination, its status is Paystack's word, and the caregiver side follows explicit rules.

- **One refund model.** `refunds` (one row per refund, many per booking over time, **one open at a time**) and `refund_attempts` (one row per Paystack call; a retry adds a row). States: `pending_approval`, `approved`, `processing`, `needs_attention`, `needs_review`, `failed` (all open), `succeeded`, `rejected`, `closed` (terminal). `failed` is **open**: it can be retried, so it keeps the booking's slot until closed.
- **Every cancel path queues a refund.** `flag_refund` (admin cancel, WhatsApp cancel by the booker, drop-out decisions) and the late-payment path create a `system` refund with the policy's suggested amount; a caregiver cancel of a paid **app** booking now queues a full refund (the gap fix; before, it returned early). Staff raise one with `POST /admin/refunds` (completed or cancelled bookings only).
- **Approval rules.** Finance approves up to `finance_refund_limit_naira` (₦50,000), **cumulative per booking**; above it a Super Admin alone (new permission `refunds.approve_above_limit`, seeded on the Super Admin role). A staff-raised refund needs a different approver. Wallet credit is Super Admin only and app bookers only.
- **Sending.** `refund_dispatch_job` (every minute) claims an approved refund, commits attempt N as `sending`, then calls Paystack with no lock held. Silence (timeout, 5xx, unreadable) leaves the attempt `unknown` and **never resends**; `refund_reconcile_job` (minutes 7, 22, 37, 52) asks Paystack and settles. `refund.pending|processing|needs-attention|processed|failed` webhooks go through the existing signature check to `refunds.apply_refund_event` (matched by Paystack's refund id, else the booking's one open refund by transaction reference; amount must match).
- **Failure handling.** `failed`: retry (asks Paystack first), switch to the wallet (Super Admin), or close (reason). `needs_attention`: Finance enters the family's bank account (`bank-details`; the account number goes to Paystack and is **never stored**; only the bank code, last four and the resolved name). Contradictions from Paystack go to `needs_review` and only a Super Admin `resolve`s them.
- **Caregiver take-back** (RF-D6), applied when the money has actually left: follows what the caregiver received. New ledger type `refund_take_back`, new `caregiver_wallets.owed_kobo`.
- **Legacy retired.** `GET/PUT /patient/admin/refund/requests` and `POST /patient/wallet/refund/request` answer `410`; the old wallet refund flow and its tests are gone.
- **Booking `payment_status`** gains `partially_refunded`; `bookings.paystack_fee_kobo` records Paystack's fee at settlement (reporting only; Oakly absorbs it).
- **Admin API** `/api/v1/admin/refunds` (list, detail, request, approve, reject, retry, bank-details, switch-to-wallet, close, resolve), `available_actions` per caller, naira at the edge, every write audited as `refund.*`.

## Why

Before this slice a cancelled paid booking was only flagged (`refund_required`); nothing paid the family back, the Finance limit was stored but unenforced, the only working refund code was the legacy wallet flow, and an app-channel caregiver cancel did not even raise the flag. The anti-goals that shaped every decision: **never pay a family twice or more than they paid**, never raise what a caregiver can be paid automatically or ask a caregiver to repay, never move refund money without a named human approver, and never show a status we guessed instead of Paystack's.

## How: owner decisions and how each was built

| ID | Decision | How it is built |
|---|---|---|
| RF-D1 | Family cancel refunds total less the tier fee; **Oakly absorbs Paystack's fee** in every case | `refunds.suggested_amount_kobo` uses the booking's frozen `cancel_policy` (never today's); the Paystack fee is never deducted; `bookings.paystack_fee_kobo` is recorded for reporting |
| RF-D2 | No-fault cancels refund every naira | `NO_FAULT_REASONS`, admin full cancel, caregiver cancel: suggestion = total |
| RF-D3 | Finance up to ₦50,000, above that a Super Admin alone | `refunds.approve_above_limit` (Super Admin only, seeded in `0014`); checked in `_approve` against the **cumulative** booking total |
| RF-D4 | Original payment method by default; Super Admin may choose wallet credit (app users only) | `destination`; `_check_wallet_owner`; wallet approval credits in the same transaction and the refund is `succeeded` at once |
| RF-D5 | Failed returns to the queue; needs-attention takes the family's bank account; family told at started / complete / bank details | `failed` open + retry/switch/close; `submit_bank_details`; events `refund_flagged` (`:processing`), `refund_processed` (`:complete`), `refund_needs_bank_details`, keyed per refund and status |
| RF-D6 | Approver decides per refund to take the caregiver's share back; wallet first, then owed; never ask a caregiver to repay | `earnings.apply_take_back`, `owed_kobo`, see rulings below |
| RF-D7 | Caregiver always keeps `late_cancel_compensation` | the checklist item "void/adjust `late_cancel_compensation`" is **decided won't-do**. The compensation is never reversed by the refund of its own booking (see the open question in the follow-ups) |
| RF-D8 | No refund is automatic; app caregiver cancel queues a full refund | every refund is `pending_approval`; `_after_caregiver_cancel` no longer returns early for the app channel |
| RF-D9 | Approver may lower the amount, never above what is refundable | `amount_naira` on approve; `refundable_kobo` re-checked under the booking lock |
| RF-D10 | Legacy refund routes retired | `410`; `process_refund_webhook` and `_compensate_refund` removed |
| RF-D11 | A staff-raised refund needs a different approver | `_check_requester`; `available_actions` hides `approve` from the raiser (the raiser may still `reject` their own request) |
| RF-D12 | The limit is cumulative per booking | `_committed_others_kobo` plus the amount vs the limit; `max_approvable_naira` exposes it |

### Rulings taken during the build that changed or refined the plan

| Ruling | Effect | Cost if wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **Take-back follows what the caregiver received.** Voided or missing earning: nothing. Unreleased (`pending_clearance`, `held`): the earning row is reduced. Released: wallet debit, then `owed_kobo`. | the preview and the applied amount are the same rule; an unreleased earning's reduction is less visible than a wallet debit | none |
| **Owed is recovered at release AND at payout reservation.** `earnings.release_due` nets `owed_kobo` first; `payouts._reserve` (the single payout choke point) also nets it, so money returned to the balance by a failed payout cannot be paid out while owed > 0. | touches `payouts.py`; guarded by the payout sequence tests | none |
| **Owed is recovered from any release, including a `late_cancel_compensation`.** RF-D7 protects a compensation from reversal by its own booking's refund, not from paying another refund's debt. | | **Owner to confirm** (see follow-ups): the owner may want compensation ring-fenced |
| **A payout retry is refused (409) while `owed_kobo > 0`.** The admin cancels the payout and the next batch nets the debt first; shrinking a payout mid-life would break attempt amounts. | one extra admin step for a caregiver with a debt | small |
| **Separation of duties on bank details.** The admin who approved or raised a refund cannot enter its bank details, and above the Finance limit bank details need `refunds.approve_above_limit` (bank details redirect money). The resolved account holder's name is **not** compared with the patient's (fuzzy; follow-up). | one more person needed for a `needs_attention` refund | small |
| **`max_approvable_naira`** on every refund and `approve` offered only when there is room under the caller's limit; Finance may approve a lower amount. | the console never offers a button that 403s | none |
| **Every refund Paystack paid is counted** (`counted_refund_clause`): a committed status OR any attempt Paystack processed, whatever the refund's local status. An outside Paystack refund (dashboard) while another refund is open is recorded as a CLOSED row with a processed attempt and a review flag; with none open, a system refund in `needs_review`. | `refundable` cannot be overstated; Finance acknowledges a flagged row with `resolve` | one click |
| **The money backstop at the total.** Retry, switch-to-wallet, the send claim and wallet approval re-check under the booking lock that this refund plus every other counted refund is within what the family paid (409 `Paying this would refund more than the family paid...`). | defence in depth against any future mis-classification | one extra sum query per money move |
| **`failed` is open and committed;** only `rejected` and `closed` free the booking. | Finance must close a failed refund before raising another | one click |
| **Staff-raised refunds are limited to COMPLETED or CANCELLED bookings** (422 otherwise). | a partial refund never leaves a live booking that dispatch, feed and cancel paths would treat as unpaid | a goodwill refund on an upcoming visit waits |
| **Retry / switch / close ask Paystack first, with no lock held, and re-read under the locks.** Close also re-asks (a 404 on our own id is accepted); a `processed` answer puts the refund in `needs_review` and refuses (409); Paystack unreachable is 503. | never proceed on silence | none |
| **A late answer to our own unconfirmed send is adopted** (a `processed` for a send that had no reply is ours, not an outside refund), even if reconcile already moved the refund to review. | no false double count | none |
| **Resolve is strict.** `failed` is refused (409) once money left; `succeeded` records the truth (runs the success routine once when `processed_at` is empty, accepts Paystack's confirmed amount via `amount_naira`, scales `take_back_kobo` to it and never above what the approver agreed). Review reasons append, never overwrite. | a real reversal is a manual matter on the Paystack dashboard | none |
| `open_system_refund` does **not** clear `booking.refund_required`; `_mark_succeeded` does. | the WhatsApp "refund being processed" notice and `my_bookings` read the flag | the legacy flag stays set while a refund is open |
| "App user with a wallet" = channel `app` and the patient's user exists and is active. | an app patient with a deactivated account cannot get wallet credit (card refund works) | small |
| Bank code, id, last 4 and name live in `refund_attempts` columns, not `refund.note`. | | none |
| Refund notices read the refund's own amount and state, not `booking.refund_required`; the keyless admin-cancel "being processed" notice was removed (a guessed status). | families hear about a refund when it is sent | small |
| Paystack bank id: Paystack wants its numeric bank id, not the bank code (`058` is bank `9`). | bank details send the id | none |
| Refund list pages with `limit`/`offset`/`total` like `/admin/payouts`; naira out as JSON numbers; create returns `201`; bank-holder fields are blanked unless the caller holds `refunds.approve` or `payments.read`. | | FE adapts from the guide |
| `require_any_permission` added to `app/api/deps.py` for the refund reads (`require_permission(*perms)` requires ALL). | | a new auth helper, reviewed |
| The final review's three fix waves deviated from the usual "one fix wave": a known double-payment path on a money system was not shipped. | one extra round | tokens |

## Verification

- **TDD, with a seeded random-sequence test and forced races:** `test_refunds_sequences.py` runs 100 random sequences of create / approve / send / webhook (any status, any attempt, duplicates, out of order) / reconcile / retry / switch / close / take-back, asserting after every step that refunds Paystack paid plus in-flight never exceed what the family paid, the caregiver side never exceeds earned, balances stay non-negative; two deliberate mutations are caught. Four forced races (double approve, approve vs webhook, two send workers, webhook vs reconcile) use real threads and row locks.
- **Reviews:** Opus per-task review on every money task (T4 to T7, T9 folded into the final review), the final whole-branch Opus review (0 Critical, 4 Important, 4 Minor; fixed in wave 1), a re-review that found one remaining Critical (a refund Paystack paid outside Oakly uncounted while another refund is open, which could let a wallet approval over-refund) and a dead end in `resolve`, fixed in wave 2, and a third re-review that found a Critical regression (a refund's own late processed answer split off as an outside refund) fixed in wave 3. Wave 3 re-review: all five items addressed, no new Critical or Important. A security scan produced the bank-detail separation of duties and the `resolve` hardening.
- **Migration:** `alembic upgrade head` to `0014_refunds_v2`, `downgrade -1`, `upgrade head` again on the live stack's Postgres 16: clean (also `test_refunds_migration.py`).

## Operate / roll back

- The worker must run. Jobs: `refund_dispatch_job` (every minute at second 40), `refund_reconcile_job` (minutes 7, 22, 37, 52). By hand, inside the worker container: `from app.db.session import SessionLocal; from app.services import refunds; refunds.dispatch_due(SessionLocal()); refunds.reconcile(SessionLocal())`. Webhook: the existing `POST /payment/paystack/webhook` (signature check unchanged); a refund event matching no v2 refund is logged and ignored (`200`).
- **Before the first real refund:** (1) run one refund end to end against a live Paystack **test** account (refund create by transaction reference, `refund.*` webhooks, the numeric bank id on `retry_with_customer_details`, `GET /refund/{id}` and `GET /refund?transaction=`); (2) confirm whether Paystack charges a fee on refunds (not stated on the page the design was read from; RF-D1 has Oakly absorb it either way); (3) watch the logs for `needs review` and `refund ... cannot be sent` errors: every contradiction is also an `error` log line.
- **Alert on:** any refund in `needs_review` or with `review_flagged_at` set; any refund `processing` for more than an hour; any refund `approved` for more than a few minutes (the send job is down).
- **Stopping refunds without a rollback:** do not approve; or reject / close the queue rows. Approval is the only gate to money moving.
- **Roll back** (`alembic downgrade -1`): drops `refunds`, `refund_attempts` and the columns, **deletes `refund_take_back` ledger rows** (the type CHECK cannot be restored while they exist, so what was taken back is no longer on the ledger), and removes the `refunds.approve_above_limit` grant. **Postgres keeps the `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED` enum value** (an enum value cannot be dropped); a booking holding it needs a manual fix before the old code reads it. Money Paystack already refunded is not undone. **Once real refunds exist, do not downgrade; write a forward migration.** (Greenfield now, so acceptable today.) `0014`'s `down_revision` is `0013_finance_audit_read` (merged in #18).

## Follow-ups

**Owner questions**
- **Compensation and owed.** Owed is recovered from any release, including a `late_cancel_compensation` (ruling above). Confirm, or ring-fence compensation.
- **Account-holder name vs the patient's name** on bank details: not enforced (the match is fuzzy). Decide whether a mismatch should warn or block.
- **Unverified JWT decode in `app/api/deps.py` `get_user_or_admin`** (pre-existing, `390d063`; flagged by Semgrep during this slice): it decodes without verifying only to read `aud` and pick the decoder that then verifies the signature. Not a bypass today, but replace the peek with `jwt.get_unverified_header`/a verified two-decoder attempt so the scanner stays clean.
- Whether Paystack charges a fee on refunds (affects RF-D1's cost, not its rule).

**Known gaps (not blocking)**
- **App-patient refund notifications.** The pipeline has no app delivery for refund events; app bookers see only the booking's `payment_status`. An app push or email is open.
- **`owed_kobo` is not shown in the caregiver app** (the wallet API shows nothing for it; the take-back row appears in the history).
- **`POST /wallet/payment` with a `booking_id`** debits the patient wallet but never marks the booking paid, so it cannot produce a refundable booking (an oddity recorded, not handled).
- **Deposit refunds.** The legacy routes are retired; refunding a wallet deposit is a follow-up if ever needed.
- **Webhook-exceptions queue** (AD-FR-29) and four-eyes approvals (AD-FR-86), CSV exports.
- **Console screens** for the refunds queue (the API contract is the FE guide section 12.12).
- **Inactive patient wallet:** `credit_refund_locked` itself credits an inactive wallet; the approve and switch paths refuse an inactive account first.
- Deferred minors from the reviews: a repeat `flag_refund` overwrites `refund_required_reason` / `refund_amount_kobo` though no new row is created; `flag_refund` silently skips the row when the booking has no session; `ix_refunds_booking` is redundant with the partial unique and status indexes; `patient_transactions.refund_id` is unindexed; fees passed raw to `paystack_fee_kobo` (filtered later) with no test for bool/negative fees; the fake's `refund_error` `422` also applies to `GET` refund routes; a refused payout retry still spends one Paystack verify call; `take_back_from_caregiver` stays true when the scaled `take_back_kobo` is 0; a succeeded refund moved to `needs_review` leaves the booking's `payment_status` and `refund_amount_kobo` as they were; with Paystack unreachable, id-less sends sorted first can fill `RECONCILE_BATCH` and starve ones with ids; the "no Paystack reference" branch sets `failed` without an attempt row or notice; `_settle_locked` truncates `review_reason` to 500 when appending; the sequence fuzzer does not count adopt-in-review, the claim backstop or the `paid_earlier` gate (unit-tested); a different-amount `processed` for our own unconfirmed send is over-counted (human-gated); `max_approvable_naira` overstates for non-pending committed refunds (approve is never offered there); the rejected-match in the closed/rejected processed-flag uses `suggested_amount_kobo`; `UniqueConstraint` import in `caregiver.py` possibly unused.

Make the first message naira.
